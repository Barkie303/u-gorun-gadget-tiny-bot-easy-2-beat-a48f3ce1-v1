# Tiny Bot, Easy $2: Beat Buy-and-Hold on Solana

A tiny paper-trading bot for hourly Solana prices. MACD(12,26,9) above its
signal line plus CCI(14) at or under -48 buys the dip in an uptrend; it exits
fast on a MACD cross down, +2% take-profit, or a -5% stop. Four slots at up
to 25% of equity each, long only, no leverage, no shorts.

Dev-window practice score (kit's `grade-local`, 136 hourly bars):
**+$138.36 net PnL** — buy-and-hold -$869.34, best house baseline -$20.22.
